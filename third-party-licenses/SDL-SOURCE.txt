This is unmodified SDL3. On desktop it is linked into the
daysengine binary; on Android it is libSDL3.so in the APK, for
the Java-to-native reason in tools/build-sdl.sh. To build against
your own copy instead, point tools/build-sdl.sh at it, or drop the
static-sdl feature and link the SDL3 your system provides.
