libavcodec has dav1d 1.5.1 (https://github.com/videolan/dav1d,
BSD 2-clause, text in DAV1D-COPYING.txt) linked into it statically.

These libraries are unmodified FFmpeg. DaysEngine links them
dynamically and ships them unchanged; replacing them with your own
build of the same soname is supported and is the point of shipping
them shared rather than static.
